# IMXO Brand Policy

This policy governs the use of the official IMXO brand assets.

It is intended to allow reasonable community, editorial, educational, and interoperability-related use of the IMXO name and visual identity while protecting the project from misleading, unauthorized, or commercial use of its brand.

This policy may be refined as the project develops.

## 1. Covered brand assets

The following are considered official IMXO brand assets unless explicitly stated otherwise:

- the **IMXO** name as the name of the project and standard;
- the official IMXO logo;
- **Kumixo** as the project mascot and brand character;
- official Kumixo artwork;
- official IMXO brand illustrations;
- other files or materials explicitly identified as official IMXO brand assets.

Brand assets are not automatically licensed under the repository's Apache License 2.0 or Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

A file-specific notice may grant additional permissions for a particular asset.

## 2. Ownership and project identity

IMXO is the project name.

Its normative expansion is:

**Image Model eXchange Object**

The technical GitHub organization name, repository names, domain names, or other infrastructure identifiers do not change the project name.

Nothing in this policy should be interpreted as a claim that a trademark is registered in a jurisdiction where no such registration exists.

## 3. Uses that are generally permitted

You may use the IMXO name and official brand assets without prior permission for reasonable non-commercial purposes such as:

- referring to or discussing IMXO;
- articles, reviews, research papers, blog posts, presentations, and news coverage;
- educational materials;
- community discussions and events;
- documentation that explains compatibility with or support for IMXO;
- screenshots or illustrations that show the IMXO project or its official materials;
- non-commercial fan art or community material, provided it does not create confusion about official status.

Attribution to the IMXO project should be provided where practical.

Permitted use must not imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement, partnership, or official affiliation when none exists.

## 4. Descriptive and compatibility use

The IMXO name may be used descriptively to explain compatibility, implementation, or support.

Examples of normally acceptable wording include:

- `supports IMXO`;
- `IMXO compatible`;
- `compatible with IMXO`;
- `IMXO viewer`;
- `IMXO parser`;
- `IMXO converter`;
- `implementation of IMXO`;
- `tool for IMXO files`.

Such wording must not make an unofficial implementation, fork, extension, product, or service appear to be the official IMXO project or an officially certified implementation unless that status has actually been granted.

## 5. Modified specifications, forks, and extensions

The IMXO technical documentation may be available under open documentation licenses, but the IMXO brand must not be used in a misleading way.

If you publish a modified specification, incompatible fork, experimental profile, or derivative design:

- clearly state that it is unofficial;
- clearly identify the modifications or extensions;
- do not present it as the official IMXO specification;
- do not use official IMXO branding in a way that suggests the project has approved or adopted the derivative work.

Descriptive references to IMXO remain permitted.

## 6. Commercial use

Prior permission is required for use of official IMXO brand assets in commercial branding or merchandise, including:

- selling clothing, stickers, posters, figurines, accessories, or other merchandise featuring the official IMXO logo or Kumixo;
- using official IMXO brand assets as a primary brand for a commercial product or service;
- commercial advertising that implies endorsement by the IMXO project;
- resale or commercial distribution of official brand artwork as artwork or merchandise;
- using Kumixo or the IMXO logo as the identity of an unrelated commercial offering.

Commercial software and services may still make normal descriptive statements such as `supports IMXO` or `compatible with IMXO` without requesting permission, provided the use is accurate and does not imply endorsement.

## 7. Prohibited or misleading uses

Do not use IMXO brand assets in a way that:

- falsely implies official approval, certification, sponsorship, or affiliation;
- impersonates the IMXO project, organization, maintainers, or official channels;
- makes an unofficial fork appear to be the official IMXO standard;
- materially alters the official logo and presents the result as an official logo;
- uses the official brand identity as the primary identity of an unrelated project;
- is deceptive, fraudulent, unlawful, or intended to confuse users about origin or ownership.

## 8. Logo and artwork modifications

You may resize official artwork for reasonable use.

For editorial, educational, and community purposes, minor layout changes required for placement are acceptable provided the result does not misrepresent the official identity.

Substantial modifications, recoloring, derivative mascot designs, or altered logos should not be presented as official IMXO artwork unless approved by the project.

Fan art and unofficial derivative artwork should be clearly recognizable as unofficial where confusion is reasonably possible.

## 9. Brand assets inside this repository

Files under:

```text
assets/brand/
```

should be treated as official brand assets unless a file-specific notice states otherwise.

They are excluded from the general Apache-2.0 and CC BY 4.0 grants that apply to other classes of repository material.

Documentation diagrams and illustrations outside `assets/brand/` may use different licensing terms as described in `LICENSES.md` or a file-specific notice.

## 10. Third-party marks

This policy applies only to IMXO-controlled brand assets.

Third-party trademarks, logos, names, fonts, artwork, or other protected materials remain subject to the rights of their respective owners.

Their presence in the repository does not grant additional rights.

## 11. Permission requests

Uses outside the permissions described above require prior permission from the IMXO project.

Until a dedicated project contact is established, use the maintainer contact information published in the repository.

When requesting permission, include:

- the asset you want to use;
- how it will be used;
- whether the use is commercial;
- where the material will appear;
- whether the asset will be modified;
- any relevant mock-up or example.

## 12. No certification implied

Use of the IMXO name to describe technical compatibility does not itself mean that an implementation has been tested, validated, certified, or endorsed by the IMXO project.

If an official conformance or certification program is introduced in the future, its marks and usage rules may be governed by additional terms.

## 13. Changes to this policy

The IMXO project may update this policy as the project, community, and brand evolve.

Changes should preserve reasonable descriptive, educational, editorial, research, and interoperability-related use of the IMXO name while protecting against misleading or unauthorized commercial use.
